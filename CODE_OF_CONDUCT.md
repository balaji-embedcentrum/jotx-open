# Code of conduct

jotx is a small project, and we want it to be a friendly one.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most disagreements are about trade-offs, not about people.
- Critique code and ideas, never the person who wrote them.
- Welcome newcomers, and answer the question that was asked.
- Respect people's time: write clear issues, and say when you can no longer work on something you picked up.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, use **Report content** in the comment's `…` menu on GitHub, which reaches GitHub and the maintainers, or contact a maintainer privately. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules. The response will fit what happened, from a reminder up to a permanent ban.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
